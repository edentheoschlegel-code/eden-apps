How it works

What happens after you pay.

No surprises between paying and receiving. Here’s exactly what happens, in order, for each offer — though checkout isn’t open yet, so the first step below isn’t live today. Send a brief anyway, and I’ll follow up directly once it is.

The Second Look

  1. 01

    You pay

    The full fee, prepaid — $950, or $1,800 with payments and store submission checked. A receipt arrives by email.

  2. 02

    You send a brief

    Right after paying, send a written brief — a link to the repo or app, and anything I should know. This starts the five-business-day clock.

  3. 03

    I review

    Every security, data-handling, and store-compliance issue gets ranked by what matters most. One round of written questions if I need something clarified — answered in writing, no call.

  4. 04

    You receive the report

    A plain-English, ranked write-up by day five. Done. If you'd like the findings fixed, the audit fee is credited toward a Quiet Build.

The Quiet Build

  1. 01

    You pay a deposit

    A deposit at booking — the rest is settled at spec sign-off, before the build starts.

  2. 02

    You send a brief

    What you have — a spreadsheet, a workaround, a half-built idea — and what you want it to become. Written, not a call.

  3. 03

    We settle the spec, in writing

    I send back a written spec: what's in scope, what's not, what it looks like when it's done. You approve it in writing before anything is built.

  4. 04

    You pay the balance

    Once the spec is approved, the remaining balance is due. The build starts after that, not before.

  5. 05

    I build, async

    No calls during the build. A recorded walkthrough — a Loom, not a meeting — replaces any check-in.

  6. 06

    You receive the build

    A finished, private-by-design app, plus one revision round if something needs adjusting. Code is yours outright once it's delivered.

  7. 07

    The Keeper Plan, if you want it

    Mentioned once, plainly, at handoff — never before. If your tool needs ongoing care, this is where that conversation starts.

The Keeper Plan

  1. 01

    You opt in

    Only offered after a Quiet Build ships, and only for tools I built. First month is paid to start.

  2. 02

    You send a request when something comes up

    One active request at a time, by email. A two-business-day response window, stated in writing — no emergency line, no unlimited anything.

  3. 03

    You pause or leave, anytime

    No retention friction. Pause a month, come back later, or leave for good — it's a month-to-month plan, not a contract.
