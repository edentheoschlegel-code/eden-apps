The brand

A leaf on a sill.

The whole identity lives in one image: a single sage leaf resting on one honest line — the windowsill of The Still Room. A small tool set down in a warm, near-empty room, with nobody else in it. The room is empty on purpose: a local-first tool has nothing to broadcast.

Tools that stay on your side.
