Privacy

Privacy Policy · Eden Fonts

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Eden Fonts works without an Eden account. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not send your designs or personal information to Eden Apps. Your projects are stored on your device unless you choose to export, share or connect a sync folder.

Your studio on this device

The app saves your editable projects, project titles and collections, words and Whisper notebook entries, font choices, colors, layouts, selected images, favorites, style kit, animation settings and the card you are writing. It also remembers preferences such as your home artwork choice. Your library has a local backup for recovery. These files and preferences stay in the app’s storage; your device’s own backup settings may include them.

Photos and logos you select

You can choose a background photo or logo with the system photo picker, or drop an image into a design. Eden Fonts receives only the image you select, not permission to browse your whole photo library. The app processes a copy on your device and saves that copy with the design or style kit. Removing the copy from Eden Fonts does not delete the original in Photos.

When you choose Save Image or Save Video through the system share sheet, the system may ask permission to add that export to Photos. Eden Fonts does not request your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Optional folder sync

In My Studio you may select a folder in iCloud Drive or another file provider supported by your device. Eden Fonts stores permission to reconnect to that chosen folder and exchanges editable project revisions there. Revisions include the text, layout and any photo or logo embedded in each project. Favorites and the separate style kit remain on each device.

Your chosen file provider handles transfer and cloud storage under its own privacy policy and your account settings. Eden Apps does not run a sync server or receive a copy. Anyone with access to a shared folder may be able to read the projects in it. Turn off sync to stop future exchanges; that action leaves existing folder copies in place.

Exporting, printing and deletion

Exports use system controls, and you choose their destination. On Mac, PNG and PDF exports use the native Save dialog. iPhone and iPad also support MP4 video exports. Sharing editable projects or licensed font files uses the system sharing options; printing uses the system print dialog. Temporary export copies may remain in the app’s temporary storage until cleaned up. Editable project files include selected images and text, so share them only with people or services you intend.

Deleting a project removes it from the local library. Existing sync revisions, exported files, Photos items, printer copies and device or cloud backups are separate copies and are not deleted automatically. Remove those copies at their destinations when needed. Removing the app’s local data does not remove external copies or installed fonts.

Installed fonts

The app asks the system which Eden font files are installed so it can show their status. This status stays on the device. On iPhone and iPad, remove installed fonts in Settings → General → Fonts; on Mac, use Font Book. You can also use Remove on the font’s page in the app.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple handles payments, renewals and restoration through StoreKit. The app reads verified purchase information to determine access to the Original Collection and Eden Fonts Pro. Eden Fonts does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password, and does not send StoreKit transactions to an Eden server. Apple’s privacy policy governs Apple’s processing of purchases.

Links and support

Opening a support, terms or privacy link visits a website outside the app’s local studio. That website may process ordinary connection information; the Eden Apps website privacy policy applies. If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you send and use them to answer your request. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

Children and changes

The app does not collect personal information from users of any age. This page will be updated when its data practices change. The date above identifies this revision.
