Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This policy covers two things: the apps — including the Mac tools (Furrow, Pagenook, Clearleaf, and Bookplate) — and Eden, for Businesses, which is the Second Look, the Quiet Build, and the Keeper Plan. The short version: the apps don’t collect anything, and what you send me is used only to do the work you asked for.

The Mac tools

Furrow, Pagenook, Clearleaf, and Bookplate run on your Mac. They have no accounts and no servers of mine to talk to, so your spreadsheets, PDFs, documents, photos, and saved articles are read on your machine and stay there. I never see them, and neither does anyone else. None of the four collects analytics, telemetry, or crash reports, and none of them sends your files anywhere.

When the Mac tools use the network

Furrow, Pagenook, and Clearleaf make no network requests at all. You can work in them with the Wi-Fi off and nothing changes.

Bookplate is the exception, because opening web pages is what it is for. When you give it a link, it loads that page the way a browser would — the site sees your visit, and any scripts that site runs are its own, not mine. When you save an article, Bookplate also fetches that article’s images so your copy still works offline later. Those requests go to the site you chose, and nowhere else. Your library itself is a file on your Mac: it is never uploaded, never synced, and never shared.

Unlocking a Mac tool

When you buy a Mac tool you receive a license code. The app checks that code on your Mac, by maths, against a key built into the app itself — nothing is sent anywhere to verify it, and unlocking works the same with the network switched off. The code is then kept in the app’s own folder on your machine. It contains no personal information: only which tool it unlocks, a random order reference, and the date it was made.

What I receive

When you send a written brief, I receive what you choose to include — a link to your repo or app, a description of what you need, and your name and email so I can reply. If you send code or an app for review or building, that’s used solely to complete the engagement you paid for.

How it’s used

Your brief, your code, and anything you send is used only to write your audit report, build your tool, or handle your care request. I don’t reuse your code, your business details, or your brief for any other purpose, and I don’t share them with anyone else.

What I don’t do

  • I don’t reuse your code or brief for other clients or projects.
  • I don’t share your business details with anyone else.
  • I don’t add you to a mailing list for contacting me.
  • I don’t sell, rent, or share your personal information.

Buying a Mac tool

Furrow, Pagenook, Clearleaf, and Bookplate are sold through FastSpring, which acts as the merchant of record — FastSpring is the seller for the transaction itself and handles the payment and the tax. You enter your payment details with them, and I never see or store your card number. What reaches me is your email address and what you bought, so I can send your license code and a receipt and help you if something goes wrong. That’s all I use it for: never marketing, never sold, never shared. The purchase happens in your browser — the app itself never sees any part of it.

Buying the other apps

Purchases of the web and iPhone apps are handled by RevenueCat and Stripe, on the same footing: you enter your details directly with them, I never see or store your full card number, and what reaches me is your email address and what you bought, used only to send your receipt and help you if you need it.

Payments for business work

Deposits and fixed fees are processed by Stripe. You enter your payment details directly with Stripe — I never see or store your full card number. To process a payment, Stripe shares limited information with me: your email address, your name (if your payment method provides it), the amount paid, and your card’s brand and last four digits. I use this only to send receipts, process refunds, and provide support — never for marketing, never sold or shared.

If you send a written brief

The intake form is delivered through Web3Forms, which passes your submission to my inbox and doesn’t use it for anything else. I use what you send only to respond to you.

How long I keep it

Briefs, code, and project files are kept only as long as needed to complete and support your engagement. If you’d like anything deleted after the work is done, email and I will.

Changes to this policy

If I update this policy, I’ll revise the date at the top of this page.
