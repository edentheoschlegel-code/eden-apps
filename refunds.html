30-day money-back guarantee

Last updated: August 5, 2026

If you're not happy with a purchase you made here, email within 30 days of your purchase date and you'll get a full refund. There are no forms to complete and no hoops to jump through.

I'll probably ask what didn't work — not because your refund depends on your answer, but because your feedback is how the next version gets better. You're never obligated to explain your decision.

What's covered

  • A full refund within 30 days of purchase, on everything sold here: the Mac tools — Furrow, Pagenook, Clearleaf, and Bookplate — the all-four bundle, and Local Invoice, LocalResume, Local PDF, and My Snowball bought on the web.
  • No explanation required.
  • No hidden conditions and no cancellation fees.
  • Whatever you already made with the tool stays yours to keep.

What's not covered

  • Requests made more than 30 days after purchase.
  • Purchases made through Apple's App Store — LocalResume, Local Invoice, Local PDF, or My Snowball on your iPhone or iPad, and any Mac tool from the Mac App Store. Apple issues those refunds under its own policy, and I have no way to issue one for you: ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. The same app bought here is covered by the thirty days above.

How the money comes back

Mac tools sold here go through FastSpring, acting as the merchant of record. The web apps are handled by RevenueCat and Stripe, and the Chrome extensions through ExtensionPay. Whichever one took your money sends it back, to the card or account you paid with — you don't need to know which, or contact them yourself. Allow about 5–10 business days for it to appear on your statement; that part is the bank's pace, not mine.

When a refund is issued, the Pro access associated with that purchase is removed. A Mac tool's licence ends with the refund too — though the code is checked offline, on your own Mac, so there's nothing for me to switch off remotely. Please stop using the tool.

Why it works this way

I believe you should only pay for products that genuinely help you. If they don't, I'd rather earn your trust than keep your money.

Contact

To request a refund, or to ask about one, email support@edenapps.app. For a Chrome extension, email extensions@edenapps.app.
