Privacy Policy · Local Detail Quote

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Local Detail Quote keeps your customers, vehicles, quotes, invoices, photos, signatures and payment records on your iPhone or iPad. There is no account, and the app sends nothing to Eden Apps or to anyone else.

Local Detail Quote is a quoting and invoicing app for mobile car detailers, made by Eden Apps. A shorter version of this policy is inside the app, under Settings and Privacy Policy.

At a glance

QuestionFor Local Detail Quote

The short version

The app has no account and no sign in. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your information to Eden Apps or to any third party. Eden Apps cannot see any of it.

What the app stores

The app stores what you enter, in its own storage:

  • your business details, logo and payment details (for example a payment link or a Cash App $cashtag)
  • your customers' names, phone numbers, email addresses and addresses
  • vehicles, service prices, quotes, jobs, invoices and payments you record
  • before and after photos you take or choose, and the notes on them
  • signatures your customers draw on the screen, with the name entered and the time they signed

What leaves your device, and when

Only what you choose to send. When you share a quote or an invoice, the app makes a PDF on the iPhone or iPad and opens the standard iOS share sheet. You pick where it goes, for example Messages or Mail, and that app handles it from there.

If your device is set to back up to iCloud or to a computer, that backup includes the app's data. Apple handles those backups under your own settings.

The app makes no network requests of its own. Buying or restoring Pro goes through Apple's App Store.

Camera and photos

The app asks for the camera only when you tap Camera to take a before or after photo. Choosing photos from your library uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you pick. Photos are kept in the app's own storage.

Purchases

Local Detail Quote Pro is currently a one-time purchase from Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. The app receives only the information StoreKit provides to confirm that your Apple Account owns Pro. Eden Apps does not receive your payment details.

Payments from your customers

The app does not take card payments. You can save your own payment link or payment handles in Settings, and the app prints them on invoices. When your customer pays through one of those services, that service's own privacy policy applies.

No tracking

There is no analytics code and no advertising code in this app, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you enter is sent to us, and we never receive it. We do not sell data. You can check this yourself: turn on Airplane Mode and the app keeps working, quotes, signatures, invoices and PDFs included, except buying or restoring Pro. Mail or Messages still need a connection to send a PDF you share, as they always do.

Children

Local Detail Quote is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Deleting your information

You can delete any quote or photo in the app, and a customer once their quotes are deleted. Deleting the app removes everything it stored from this device. A device backup you made earlier still holds a copy until that backup is replaced or deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date, and anything new that uses the network will be described here before it ships.

Contact

Write to support@edenapps.app with any privacy question.
