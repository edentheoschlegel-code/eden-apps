The Quiet Build

$9,500

A private, local-first tool, built once, shipped in weeks.

2–3 weeks for the essential build. Extended builds are prepared and submitted to the store — the review clock after that belongs to Apple, not me.

The Keeper Plan

$350–$600/mo

Capped, pausable care for a tool I built you.

Ongoing, month to month. Pause or leave anytime.

$350/month for simple tools, $600/month for store-listed apps with billing. Pause any month. Only available for tools I built, and kept to 8 clients, with a gentle waitlist after — nothing called 'unlimited', no emergency line. Every app I sell is still buy-once, no subscriptions — this is the one quiet exception, and it's optional, capped, and only for those who ask for it.
