What it does

Eden Desk is a private assistant for your own things: it scans pages, reads and fills PDFs, finds your files, keeps your notes, and adds to your calendar. It runs on your device. What you make with it is yours, and the app keeps no copy of anything anywhere else.

The free trial and the purchase

Eden Desk opens with a free trial of fourteen days. The trial is a real App Store item at no charge, it ends on its own, and nothing is charged when it does. Before the trial starts, the app shows you how long it lasts, what stops at the end, and the price of the purchase.

When the trial ends, everything you made stays yours. You can still open, read, search, copy, export and print the notes, scans and PDFs you already have. Only new work (a new note or scan, a new event or reminder, filling in a form) waits for the purchase, and the app says so in one line beside those actions.

One purchase opens the whole Local desk for good. It is one payment, it does not renew, it is tied to your Apple Account, and it covers iPhone, iPad and Mac together. Family Sharing is on, so the people in your family group can use it too.

Refunds are handled by Apple under their own policy, not by us.

Individual Cloud

Individual Cloud is a subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through the App Store. There is no free trial: three short tries of Cloud come before it, with no card and nothing to cancel. It renews on its own until you cancel with Apple. The monthly allowance of pages and answers counts from the day you first subscribed and renews on that day each month, for yearly subscriptions too, so a year holds twelve allowances. What is not used in a month does not carry over. Cancelling turns off renewal, and Cloud stays on until the end of the period you already paid for. Subscribing sends nothing: Cloud stays off until you switch it on, and nothing leaves your device until you send an item after seeing it. Each month includes up to 1,000 cloud-processed pages and 200 cloud answers; there is no overage charge, and when either is used up cloud sends stop until the allowance renews, on the day of the month you first subscribed, while everything on your device keeps working. When the subscription ends, everything on your device stays yours and Local keeps working as before.
