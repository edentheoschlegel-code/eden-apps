Eden Fonts Support

A little help for your words, your fonts and the things you make with them. If you need a hand, email support@edenapps.app.

Start with the Originals

The Original Collection has six font families: Caspienne, Rosin, Opalune, Seliora, Dune and Noctavelle. Rosin Bold, Rosin Italic and Opalune Semibold bring the collection to nine styles. All nine are free to use inside the studio, including finished-design exports. A separate one-time purchase adds installation and export of their .ttf font files for compatible apps on your own devices.

Explore a little further

Eden Fonts Pro is a separate monthly or yearly subscription. It adds 47 fonts for creating in Craft, including the five Garden fonts: Roseviane, Daisavelle, Wisteravyn, Lilaveth and Poppivelle. An active plan also includes Pro font installation, font-file export and finished-design exports using Pro fonts. You can preview Pro specimens before choosing a plan.

Open More → Choose your collection to see the Original and Pro offers. They are separate purchases. Character coverage varies by font; Florielle is a decorative uppercase A–Z alphabet.

Make something personal

Open Craft, enter your words and choose the typefaces, canvas, composition and colors. For a starting point, open My Studio → Templates and choose one of twelve editable designs. Supporting words & pairing adds a second font. Paper, color & details includes selected photos, borders and paper finishes.

My Studio holds your saved projects, collections, templates and style kit. Changes save automatically on this device. Use Undo and Redo while editing. Export & share lets you keep an editable project copy; My Studio → Import project opens one you previously saved. Whisper gives your own writing a place in the studio, too.

Keep it. Share it. Put it on paper.

In Craft, choose Export & share, then Picture · PNG or Print · PDF. On Mac, Export PNG and Export PDF open the native Save dialog. On iPhone and iPad, use the system sharing options to choose where your file goes.

Physical paper sizes produce PNGs at 300 pixels per inch and PDFs at the chosen dimensions. With Print · PDF selected, Print this design opens the print options. Use 100% scale and turn off fit-to-page scaling when you want the chosen physical size; printer margins still apply.

In Craft, Explore this design → Animate opens Living Letters. Choose movement and timing, then preview your words in motion. On iPhone and iPad, Save video exports an MP4. If a font cannot draw a character in your design, the app keeps your original text and explains what needs attention before export.

Use a font in another app

Open a font in Soul and choose its installation option. Font-file installation and export require access to that collection. On iPhone and iPad, confirm the system installation request and check Settings → General → Fonts. On Mac, check Font Book. Reopen the destination app if its font list has not refreshed.

Export the font file shares the .ttf file so you can save it or move it to your own device. Installed fonts work in compatible apps with custom-font support. They do not change the system interface font or replace your keyboard. The terms explain personal and commercial use.

Purchases and your Pro plan

Restore Purchases in More checks purchases for the Apple Account currently signed in to the App Store. If an offer has no price, check your connection and use Reload available offers.

Manage Subscription appears in the Pro offer screen when your subscription is active. You can also manage or cancel through your Apple Account; Apple’s cancellation instructions show the steps for each device. Deleting Eden Fonts does not cancel a subscription.

After Pro ends, your saved projects remain available. New Pro font installations, font-file exports and finished-design exports using Pro fonts require an active plan. Change a design to Original Collection fonts to export it for free. Font files legitimately installed or exported while subscribed remain licensed for your own devices and design work.

Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com. When emailing us for help, include your app version and what happened. Please leave out Apple Account passwords and payment details.

Bring your studio between devices

Optional folder sync in My Studio lets you choose the same iCloud Drive folder on each device, then use Sync now. Project revisions include their text and any photos or logos used in the design. Conflicting edits are kept as separate copies. Favorites and the separate style kit stay on each device.

If the folder is unavailable, your local work stays saved. Turning off sync or deleting a local project leaves existing folder copies in place. Remove those copies separately when needed, and keep editable exports of important work before removing the app.

Your words stay yours

No Eden account is required. Eden Fonts has no ads, analytics or tracking. Your work is stored locally; sharing and optional folder sync use destinations you choose. Read the privacy policy for details.
