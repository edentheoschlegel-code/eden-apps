Eden Tabs ("the Extension", formerly Tab Manager) is a Chrome extension for saving workspaces of tabs, pausing and resuming work, writing notes, searching, and keeping recovery history and backups. This policy explains what data it handles and how.

Summary

  • Eden Tabs Core, the trial, and earlier versions: your tabs, workspaces, notes, recovery history, settings, and backups are stored in this browser. Nothing from your collection leaves your computer.
  • Eden Tabs Plus, only if you connect sync: your collection is also copied to your own Google Drive, encrypted on your computer with a passphrase only you know. Google stores only the encrypted file. Eden Apps never receives it.
  • There is no Eden account, no analytics or advertising, no AI service, and nothing is shared or sold.

What the Extension stores in your browser

Using your browser's own storage (IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local):

  • Pages you choose to save: address, title, tab group name and color, pinned state, order, tags, your notes and checklists, why you saved the page, and any text you selected and chose to attach
  • Comparisons you create, and the outcome you record when you finish a project
  • Workspaces (with any reminder you set and its note), folders, templates, auto-sorts, and your Read later list with any reminder times
  • Recovery history: snapshots of the addresses and titles of your open tabs and windows, and earlier versions of your library, kept for the period you choose in Settings
  • A short list of recent changes you can undo, and a list of deleted records (kept 180 days) so a deletion can reach your other computers if you use sync
  • Your settings, and your plan status (trial start date, and whether you used an earlier version)

Private (incognito) windows are never read, saved, snapshotted, exported, synced, or logged. The Extension does not save new pages from sites you add to Excluded sites; pages from those sites that you saved earlier stay in your collection until you remove them (the Extension offers to remove them when you exclude a site).

If you choose to keep a window saved to a workspace, the Extension saves the addresses and titles of that window's tabs as they change, until the window closes or Chrome quits. Which tab belongs to which saved page is kept only in the browser's temporary session storage.

When you copy links or share a workspace as a page, the Extension puts the text on your clipboard or saves a file on your computer. Nothing is uploaded. A shared page contains the page titles and links, plus only the notes you choose to include.

Cloud sync (Eden Tabs Plus, optional)

Sync is off until you choose Connect Google Drive in Settings, Plan & sync.

  • What is uploaded: one encrypted file with your workspaces, folders, saved pages with their notes and tags, templates, auto-sorts, Read later list, saved views, comparisons, and the list of deletions.
  • What is not uploaded: settings, recovery history, the list of recent changes, and open tabs. Pages you saved before excluding their site stay in your collection and sync until you remove them.
  • Encryption: the file is compressed and encrypted on your computer with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your passphrase with PBKDF2-SHA-256 (600,000 rounds). The passphrase is never stored or sent. The derived key is kept on each computer you connect, in a form that cannot be read out.
  • Where it goes: a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only Eden Tabs can open (Google's drive.appdata access). Eden Tabs cannot see your other Drive files. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Google account.
  • Sign-in: you sign in with Google in a Google window. Eden Tabs receives a short-lived access token from Google, keeps it in memory only, and revokes it when you turn sync off.
  • When: about a minute after a change, every 15 minutes, and when Chrome starts, while sync is on.
  • Turning it off: you can turn sync off on one computer, or turn it off and delete the cloud copy from your Drive.
  • If you forget your passphrase, nobody can read the cloud copy, including Eden Apps. Your data on each computer is not affected.

What the Extension never does

  • Read the contents of the pages you visit (only titles and addresses of tabs)
  • Send your collection to Eden Apps or anyone else
  • Load remote fonts, icons, scripts, or analytics
  • Track you across the web

Backups

Backups are files you create yourself, saved where you choose (Downloads, a file, or a folder you pick). Password-protected backups are encrypted on your computer with AES-256-GCM; the password is never stored, and a forgotten password cannot be recovered by anyone.

Plans and payments

New installs can start a 7-day trial of Eden Tabs Core when you choose it; it never starts by itself. Until then, and after it, your collection stays readable and exportable; saving and editing need a plan. People who used an earlier version keep the basics free. Eden Tabs Core is a one-time purchase; Eden Tabs Plus is a monthly or yearly subscription. Earlier one-time Pro purchases remain valid.

Payments are handled by ExtensionPay using Stripe. To know your plan, the Extension asks ExtensionPay using an anonymous key that ExtensionPay creates when you open checkout. The request contains nothing about your tabs, notes, or browsing. You buy, update your card, switch plans, or cancel on ExtensionPay's and Stripe's pages; the Extension never sees your card. ExtensionPay keeps a small record of your purchase in your browser's sync storage so your plan works on your signed-in browsers. The Extension also keeps your trial start date there, so reinstalling does not restart the trial.

Permissions

  • tabs: read the title and address of open tabs so you can save, search, and review duplicates.
  • tabGroups: save and rebuild Chrome tab groups.
  • storage and unlimitedStorage: keep your library and recovery history in your browser without them being cleared for space.
  • sidePanel: show the compact side panel.
  • alarms: bring back Read later reminders, take snapshots, sync when Cloud sync is on, and run optional tab sleeping.
  • contextMenus: adds "Save page to Eden Tabs…" and "Save page with this text to Eden Tabs…" to the right-click menu. When you use the second on selected text, Chrome passes that text to the Extension; the Extension does not read the page.
  • Optional, asked only when you turn the feature on: favicon (site icons from Chrome's own local cache), sessions (Chrome's recently closed list in Recovery), notifications (Read later reminders), identity (Google sign-in for Cloud sync).
  • Network access: extensionpay.com for plans and payments; Google (accounts.google.com, www.googleapis.com, oauth2.googleapis.com) only when you connect Cloud sync.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays in your browser until you delete it in the Extension or uninstall the Extension. Uninstalling removes everything the Extension stored in the browser, including recovery history. If you used Cloud sync, the encrypted copy stays in your Google Drive until you delete it (Settings, Plan & sync, Turn off sync, with the delete option) or remove Eden Tabs' access in your Google account. Eden Apps has no copy of your data.

Children's privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes
