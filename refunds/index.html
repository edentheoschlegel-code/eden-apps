Refund Policy

30-day money-back guarantee

Last updated: July 26, 2026

If you’re not happy with your purchase, email me within 30 days of your purchase date and I’ll refund you in full. There are no forms to complete and no hoops to jump through.

I’ll probably ask what didn’t work — not because your refund depends on your answer, but because your feedback is how the next version gets better. You’re never obligated to explain your decision.

What’s covered

  • A full refund within 30 days of purchase, on everything sold here. That is the same thirty days for the Mac tools — Furrow, Pagenook, Clearleaf, and Bookplate — and the all-four bundle, and for Local Invoice, Local Resume, Local PDF, and Snowball bought on the web.
  • No explanation required.
  • No hidden conditions and no cancellation fees.
  • Whatever you already made with the tool stays yours to keep.

What’s not covered

  • Requests made more than 30 days after purchase.
  • Purchases made through Apple’s App Store. If you bought Local Invoice, Local Resume, Local PDF, or Snowball on your iPhone or iPad, Apple handles that refund under its own policy and I have no way to issue it. The same app bought here on the web is covered by the thirty days above.
  • Business engagements — the Second Look, the Quiet Build, and the Keeper Plan — which have their own terms, set out in the Terms of Service.

How the money comes back

The Mac tools are sold through FastSpring, acting as the merchant of record. The web apps are handled by RevenueCat and Stripe. Either way, the refund is issued back through whichever handled your purchase, to the payment method you used — you don’t need to know which one, or contact them yourself. Allow a few days for it to appear on your statement; that part is the bank’s pace, not mine.

Why it works this way

I believe you should only pay for products that genuinely help you. If they don’t, I’d rather earn your trust than keep your money.

Contact

To request a refund, or to ask about one, email support@edenapps.app.
