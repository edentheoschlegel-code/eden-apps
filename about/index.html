About

Why Eden, for Businesses exists

I didn’t set out to build a service business. I set out to build software that respects the person using it — no accounts you don’t need, no data leaving your device, no subscription quietly renewing in the background. That’s what became Eden Apps: four store-approved apps and nine browser extensions, each one built, tested, and supported by me alone.

Along the way, people started asking a different question. Not “can I buy this app,” but “can you build me something like it — for my business.” A founder stuck with an app someone else had rushed together. A small business owner who’d been quoted a large sum and a long timeline for something that should have taken far less. Someone who didn’t need a team, an agency, or a retainer — just one careful person who’d actually ship.

Eden, for Businesses answers that honestly, in the same way I built the apps: a published price instead of a quote, a written brief instead of a sales call, and a real person’s name on the work instead of an account manager between you and whoever’s actually doing it.

The problem it solves

Most software gets built one of three ways: a big agency that’s slow and expensive, a freelancer marketplace where you’re rolling dice on who shows up, or a no-code tool that gets you 80% of the way and quietly hits a wall. Meanwhile, a lot of AI-assisted apps are shipping fast and unreviewed — nobody checked what happens to the data, or whether the payment flow actually holds up.

This exists for the gap in between: a single, accountable person, with a visible body of shipped work, who builds privately and prices plainly — for the founder or small business that wants something built right, once, without becoming someone’s ongoing client roster.

Why choose it

  • You can verify the work before you hire. Every claim I make about how I build — private by design, no accounts, no tracking — is already true of four store-approved apps and nine extensions you can go use right now. I’m not asking you to trust a portfolio site; I’m pointing at software people actually pay for today.
  • The price is the price. No discovery call to find out what it “really” costs. The Second Look, the Quiet Build, and the Keeper Plan are each published, in writing, before you send anything.
  • Nothing is billed by the hour. You’re not paying for my calendar. You’re paying for a finished, defined thing.
  • What you get is yours. Code you own outright. No lock-in to a platform, a retainer, or me.

I couldn’t do this alone, and I don’t pretend otherwise. I work with AI as a technical collaborator — the same way I build every Eden Apps product — which lets one person do the work a small team would. But every decision, every line that ships, and every responsibility that comes with it is mine.

Who it’s for — and who it isn’t

It’s for the founder who built something fast and wants a second, careful look before real users touch it. It’s for the small business with a workaround or a spreadsheet that’s become the whole operation, ready to become a real, private tool. It’s for someone who wants a single point of accountability, not a project manager relaying messages from a team they’ll never meet.

It’s not for teams who need a dedicated project manager, an in-house hire, or someone on call for emergencies. If that’s what you need, I’ll tell you so plainly — a bad fit said early costs nothing; said late, it costs both of us.

I build one thing at a time, for one person who needs it, and I put my name on all of it.

How it started

Eden Apps began July 3, 2026, with four apps built quickly and carefully by one person. Eden, for Businesses started July 19, 2026, as the same idea aimed at businesses instead of individuals — not a pivot, not a rebrand, just the next room in the same house.
